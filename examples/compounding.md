# Example: Compounding growth system

This abbreviated example shows the expected shape of a `$compound-x-account` result. The account and metrics are fictional.

## Input

- Three healthy series across four review cycles
- Reliable conversion from profile visits into newsletter subscriptions
- Core audience: operations leaders at growing software companies
- Opportunity: convert recurring workflow analysis into durable authority
- Risk: 68% of qualified subscriptions originate from one series

## Stage fit

**Compounding — high confidence.** Multiple series and a working conversion path exist. Concentration is the main resilience risk.

## One objective

Deepen authority by turning the best workflow evidence into a quarterly field guide, while testing one adjacent problem for the same audience.

## Adjacency ladder

1. Same audience, new problem: from workflow handoffs to operating reviews.
2. New format, same promise: a sourced Article and accessible worksheet.
3. Same problem, adjacent audience: test one version for customer-success leaders only after step 1 repeats.

Do not test all three at once.

## Durable asset

- Asset: `The 2026 Handoff Failure Field Guide`
- Evidence: 12 anonymised workflow reviews with documented permission and methodology.
- Article job: structured argument, findings, counterexamples, and implementation checklist.
- Feed support: individual findings that remain useful without clicking; chart explanations; a disagreement post; a revision after reader evidence.
- Accessibility: text summaries for charts, alt text, readable labels, and captions for any supporting clips.

Article access and specifications must be rechecked against current official guidance before production.

## Collaboration rule

Invite one operations practitioner and one adjacent functional leader because they add different evidence. Agree on attribution, approval, and reuse before publishing. Do not coordinate engagement or select partners by follower count alone.

## Conversion path

Useful feed finding → profile promise → field-guide landing page → optional newsletter. Measure qualified completion and replies, not impressions alone.

## Resilience and regression

- Concentration trigger: no single series should silently become the only source of qualified conversion.
- Trust trigger: reopen evidence review after a correction, note, or substantiated challenge.
- Production trigger: pause the asset if it degrades the proven feed work.
- Regression: return the adjacent problem to Learning if it fails repeated audience-quality checks.

No part of this plan assumes Premium, Article format, or collaboration creates a direct Home-ranking boost.
