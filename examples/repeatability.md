# Example: Repeatability system

This abbreviated example shows the expected shape of a `$systemize-x-account` result. The account and metrics are fictional.

## Input

- 52 posts measured on comparable 48-hour windows
- Two mechanisms have beaten the account median in four or more executions
- Primary audience: technical founders buying their first marketing system
- Constraint: successful posts take too long to recreate and the profile promise is broader than the audience attracted

## Stage fit

**Repeatability — high confidence.** Two audience-aligned mechanisms have repeated. The next job is to formalise their grammar, protect novelty, and connect attention to the right conversion path.

## Pattern card: Build-log teardown

- Audience problem: founders cannot see why a marketing workflow breaks in practice.
- Promise: one real workflow, one failure point, one repair.
- Recognisable elements: concrete starting state, visible constraint, decision, before/after consequence.
- Required variation: new workflow, evidence, failure mode, and lesson.
- Why people may save or share: the post gives a reusable diagnostic.
- Evidence standard: creator-owned screenshot, log, or reproducible example.
- Median outcome: 1.8× account impression median and 2.1× qualified-reply median across five runs.
- Production cost: about 90 minutes with asset capture.
- Fatigue trigger: two consecutive credible runs below the relevant median or repeated audience comments that the lesson feels familiar.

## Named series

### Where the workflow broke

Grammar:

1. show the intended workflow;
2. isolate the actual bottleneck;
3. explain the false assumption;
4. show the smallest repair;
5. state when the repair does not apply.

This is a reusable mechanism, not a fill-in-the-blank wording template.

### One screen, one decision

Use one interface or dashboard view to explain a marketing decision. Vary the company stage, metric, visual, decision, and counterexample.

## Portfolio for the next cycle

- Proven: both series, only when new evidence exists.
- Adjacent: apply the same diagnostic method to onboarding and retention workflows.
- Exploratory: one short narrated screen recording testing whether motion improves explanation.

The allocation should be set from available evidence and production capacity, not a universal percentage.

## Conversion repair

Rewrite the bio and pin around `practical marketing systems for technical founders`, matching the audience already responding. The destination should open with the same promise and a clear first useful asset.

Profile conversion is a human funnel finding, not proof of a direct ranking input.

## Review rule

Review every four weeks or after six new executions, whichever comes first. Move a series to `Watch` when its audience quality, hit rate, or production economics weaken; return an uncertain mechanism to Learning.
