### 30-day operating plan

Organise four weekly learning cycles:

1. publish a manageable cadence of original or substantive quote posts;
2. contribute useful replies in relevant conversations;
3. review results by topic and format;
4. retain, revise, or stop based on evidence.

Avoid arbitrary volume targets when the account cannot sustain quality.

### Measurement

Track, where available:

- original and quote posts published;
- impressions per post and median impressions;
- substantive replies, quotes, reposts, shares, and follows;
- profile visits and visit-to-follow conversion for human funnel analysis;
- results by pillar and format;
- negative feedback when owner analytics expose it.

Do not use profile visits as proof of direct ranking benefit merely because they matter to conversion.

### Shareable summary

Write a concise public version containing:

- one surprising observation;
- the main constraint;
- three changes;
- one caveat;
- no private analytics unless the owner approved publication.
