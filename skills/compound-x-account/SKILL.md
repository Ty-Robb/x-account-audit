# Compound X Account Growth

Expand a proven account through durable assets, audience adjacency, collaborations, and conversion while protecting what earned trust.

## Accept useful evidence

Prefer:

- at least two proven content series across multiple review cycles;
- post-level outcomes, returning-engagement evidence, audience quality, and conversion data;
- profile, pinned post, destinations, offers, collaborations, production resources, and constraints;
- any owner-supplied negative-feedback, safety, or account-health evidence.

Public evidence is sufficient for a lower-confidence plan. Never request credentials or infer private platform labels.

## Load the stage method

Read [references/compounding-playbook.md](references/compounding-playbook.md) before recommending scale. Use its strategy choice, portfolio, expansion ladders, collaboration rules, governance, concentration checks, and regression triggers.

## Use persistent state when present

If `.x-growth/` exists, read `workspace.json`, `account.md`, `ledger.csv`, `series.md`, `experiments.md`, and `decisions.md` before choosing a compounding objective.

For an ongoing workspace, record portfolio choices, adjacency tests, durable assets, partnerships, concentration risks, and regression triggers. Do not store private messages or collaborator data that is not necessary for the strategy. Re-read target files before editing and preserve user changes. The skill must also work without persistent state.

## Follow the workflow

### 1. Confirm stage fit

Require multiple repeatable, audience-aligned series or reliable conversion. Do not classify an account as Compounding because it has many followers, one viral post, or high posting volume.

If the account cannot reproduce useful outcomes, move the recommendation back to Repeatability or Learning.

### 2. Choose one compounding objective

Prioritise one primary objective per cycle:

- deepen authority with durable research or teaching;
- expand into one adjacent audience or problem;
- increase qualified conversion to an owned destination;
- build a collaboration or community network;
- create a new high-value format or recurring event.

Tie every new format and partnership to that objective.

### 3. Design the portfolio

Balance proven franchises, adjacent applications, high-upside experiments, relationship content, and durable assets. Set allocations from evidence, audience risk, production capacity, and opportunity—not a generic formula.

Use Articles, video, tools, research, spaces, events, or collaborations only when the audience job warrants their cost. Build feed posts that make each deeper asset understandable and valuable without resorting to empty promotion.

### 4. Expand without dilution

Use an adjacency ladder: same audience/new problem, same problem/adjacent audience, new format/same promise, then broader category. Test one adjacency at a time and retain the account's recognisable point of view.

Select collaborators for audience relevance, complementary proof, and additive insight. Do not use coordinated engagement or reciprocal amplification as a ranking hack.

### 5. Protect trust and resilience

Audit claim quality, sourcing, accessibility, negative feedback, quoted or linked context, production bottlenecks, partner concentration, and dependence on one series. Establish a regression trigger that returns weakening areas to a Repeatability or Learning cycle.

### 6. Deliver the compounding system

Return, in this order:

1. stage-fit finding and confidence;
2. chosen compounding objective;
3. portfolio and adjacency map;
4. durable-asset or collaboration plan;
5. next 15 feed posts supporting the strategy;
6. conversion path and owned-asset plan;
7. governance and trust checks;
8. scorecard, concentration risks, and regression triggers.

## Keep evidence honest

Do not promise that Premium, a collaboration, a format, or a larger audience directly increases Home ranking. Published visibility and abuse systems make trust and safe linking material constraints, while exact runtime labels remain private unless the owner supplies an eligible report.
